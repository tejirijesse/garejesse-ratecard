02

HOW WE WORK / THE PROCESS

No account managers. No scope-creep addendums. The treatment is the contract.

  1. 01

    The first call is fifteen minutes.

    You tell me about the brand. I tell you whether the studio is the right fit — and if it isn’t, I’ll point you to two or three studios that are. That happens about forty percent of the time, and it’s the most important fifteen minutes of every project.

  2. 02

    A treatment within 48 hours.

    Two to three pages: the vision, the deliverables, the timeline, the price. No surprise line-items. The treatment is the contract.

  3. 03

    50% to confirm, 50% on delivery.

    We schedule the shoot and assemble a vetted local crew in the market we’re shooting in — we never fly a Lagos crew to Nairobi when there’s a brilliant crew already on the ground.

  4. 04

    We shoot. We edit. We grade. We mix. We deliver.

    Inside the window — 3 weeks for Founder Films, longer for Documentaries — I’d rather miss a deadline by 48 hours than ship a film that isn’t finished.

  5. 05

    Then we talk about what’s next.

    The studio’s best clients hire us once and keep hiring us. Most Founder Films become Quarterly Partnerships.

03

TERMS / THE FINE PRINT

Short, because there isn’t much of it.

Deposit

50% to confirm the booking, 50% on delivery. Quarterly Partnerships are invoiced on the 1st of each month.

Revisions

One round of director-led revisions is included. The treatment defines scope; we don’t do committee notes.

Travel

Shoots outside Nairobi are quoted with transport, accommodation and a local crew — built into the treatment, never a surprise.

Rights

You receive full usage rights to the delivered films on final payment. The studio retains the right to show the work in its portfolio.

Capacity

A finite number of projects per year, by design. Booking is first-confirmed, first-scheduled.

Currency

Priced in USD. Kenyan and continental clients can be invoiced in KES at the prevailing rate — just ask.
