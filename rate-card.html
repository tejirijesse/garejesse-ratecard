03

THE DETAIL / WHAT YOU GET

What a deliverable is — and how travel works.

What counts as a deliverable

A deliverable is a finished, colour-graded, sound-mixed, exported piece of work — suitable for publication, delivered in the formats and lengths specified in your package. That includes hero films (60–120 seconds), social cutdowns (15–30 seconds, vertical and horizontal), portrait series (10–15 stills per session), and mid-format edits (3–5 minute behind-the-scenes, founder interviews, or process films).

It does not include raw footage, intermediate revisions, planning documents, or behind-the-scenes captures we record during shoots. Those are bonus material delivered alongside every project — not counted against your quota. Every package delivers complete, finished work. We don’t ship rough cuts.

Travel & multi-market production

Gare Jesse Production is based in Kigali, Rwanda. Production within Rwanda is included at all tier prices.

For shoots in Lagos, Cape Town, Accra, Kampala, Addis Ababa, Dakar, and other African markets, travel and crew assembly are billed at a transparent flat rate per market — typically $1,500–3,500 per shoot day depending on location and crew complexity. Travel costs are quoted before the shoot, never after. No surprises. The Annual tier includes preferential multi-market scheduling — we plan continent-wide shoots in production blocks to minimise travel and maximise shoot days.

HOW IT WORKS / FOUR STEPS

No account managers. The treatment is the contract.

  1. 01

    Discovery call.

    A 15-minute conversation. You tell us about the brand. We tell you whether Gare Jesse Production is the right partner. We turn down about 40% of inquiries on this call — sometimes the budget is wrong, sometimes the brief is wrong, sometimes another studio is a better fit. We’ll say so.

  2. 02

    Treatment.

    Within 48 hours of a fit-aligned discovery call, we send a two-page treatment: the creative vision, the deliverables, the timeline, the price for your specific engagement.

  3. 03

    Agreement.

    50% invoiced on signature. Production begins. The director leads every project — never handed down to a producer or account manager.

  4. 04

    Delivery.

    Every monthly batch delivered within 3 weeks of the corresponding shoot day. Final 50% invoiced on first delivery.

FREQUENTLY / ASKED

The objections, pre-empted.

Why retainers only?

Because cinema is built through relationship, not transaction. The films we’re proud of are made with brands we know — clients we’ve spent enough time with to understand their voice, their team, their audience. One-off projects don’t build that. Retainers do.

Can I upgrade between tiers?

Yes. Many Quarterly clients move to Standard at the 3-month renewal. Many Standard clients move to Annual at the 6-month renewal. You’re never locked into a tier — only into the commitment length of your current package.

What if I want more deliverables in a month than my tier covers?

Simple: we add additional deliverables at a per-piece rate, or you upgrade tiers. We’ll always recommend the more economical option for your needs.

Do you work with brands outside Africa?

Occasionally. The studio is built around African brands and stories, but we work with international brands when the project is rooted on the continent — African market-entry films, diaspora storytelling, foundations with African programs. We don’t take work that asks us to shoot in places we don’t know.

What does the discovery call cost?

Nothing. The first conversation is always free, and frequently ends with us saying Gare Jesse Production isn’t the right fit and recommending another studio. We’d rather be a no than a wrong yes.

Are these prices negotiable?

The package architecture isn’t. The line items inside a treatment (travel, additional deliverables, language versions) are scoped to your project and discussed openly. We don’t discount packages, and we don’t enter competitive bidding processes — both undermine the work and the relationship.

What’s your refund policy?

There isn’t one. Retainer payments are non-refundable once a shoot day has been scheduled. We don’t take payment before we know we can deliver, and we deliver everything we commit to.
